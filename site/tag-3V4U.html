    Decimation increases the mean square of a child and creates a positive correlation between two children

    proposition · 3V4U · in Critical exponents from the renormalisation group

    Let $\mu$ be a symmetric law on $\mathbb R$ with variance $v\in(0,\infty)$, and let $\nu_b$ on $\mathbb R^2$ be the product $\mu\otimes\mu$ tilted by $e^{bxy}$, $b\geq0$. Then

    $$\int x^2\,d\nu_b\ \geq\ v\qquad\text{and}\qquad \int xy\,d\nu_b\ \geq\ v\tanh(bv).$$
    Proof

    Write $X=\varepsilon A$, $Y=\eta B$ with $\varepsilon,\eta$ independent fair signs independent of the amplitudes $A=\lvert X\rvert$, $B=\lvert Y\rvert$. Summing over the signs, the amplitude pair has the law proportional to $\cosh(bAB)\,\mu_A\otimes\mu_B$, and the two quantities are

    $$\int x^2d\nu_b=\frac{\mathbb E[A^2\cosh(bAB)]}{\mathbb E\cosh(bAB)},\qquad \int xy\,d\nu_b=\frac{\mathbb E[AB\sinh(bAB)]}{\mathbb E\cosh(bAB)}.$$

    On the ordered quadrant, $\partial_A\partial_B\log\cosh(bAB)=b^2\operatorname{sech}^2(bAB)\geq0$, so the tilted amplitude law is log-supermodular and the FKG inequality applies. Since $A^2$ and $A^2B^2$ are coordinatewise nondecreasing, the first display gives $\int x^2d\nu_b\geq\mathbb E[A^2]=v$ and $\int x^2y^2d\nu_b\geq v^2$. Differentiating the correlation $c(b)=\int xy\,d\nu_b$ in $b$ gives $c'(b)=\int x^2y^2d\nu_b-c(b)^2\geq v^2-c(b)^2$, and $y(b)=v\tanh(bv)$ solves $y'=v^2-y^2$ with $y(0)=0=c(0)$, so the Riccati comparison gives $c\geq y$. All laws are of bounded support, so the differentiations are legitimate.

    Now apply this to two children of a block. If every level-$n$ coupling is removed except the one between those two children, their joint law is exactly $\nu_b$ with $\mu$ the free law of $S_{n-1}$, $v=V_{n-1}$ and $b=\beta J_n$. Restoring the remaining couplings increases $\mathbb E[S_a^2]$ and $\mathbb E[S_aS_{a'}]$, because both are sums of pair correlations $\tau(x,y)\geq0$ and each of those is increasing in the couplings by Griffiths' first inequality. Hence, with $V_n=\mathbb E_\beta[S_n^2]$ and $m=L^d$,

    $$V_n=\sum_a\mathbb E[S_a^2]+\sum_{a\neq a'}\mathbb E[S_aS_{a'}]\ \geq\ mV_{n-1}\bigl[1+(m-1)\tanh(\beta J_nV_{n-1})\bigr].\tag{1}$$

    The inequality is a lower bound on $V_n$ given $V_{n-1}$, and it is the whole engine of what follows: it says that if the block spin is large then it is amplified, because the children are pulled into alignment and their cross correlations add to their own squares.
