    Ultrametric block system and its levels

    definition · U4PM · in Ultrametric block systems and the p-adic models

    Let $G$ be an abelian group and $G=G_0\supset G_1\supset G_2\supset\cdots$ a decreasing sequence of subgroups, each of finite index in $G$, with

    $$[G_n:G_{n+1}]=m\qquad\text{for every }n\geq0 .$$

    The level of a pair $x\neq y$ is the least $n\geq1$ with $x-y\in G_n$, and $\operatorname{lev}(x)=0$ only for $x=0$. The block at $x$ of level $n$ is the coset $x+G_n$; the children of a block $x+G_n$ are its cosets of $G_{n+1}$. Vertices at level $n$ are those in $G_n\setminus G_{n-1}$, and the shell at level $n$ is $G_n\setminus G_{n-1}$.

    Two examples carry the subject. For the hierarchical lattice $\mathbb H^d_L$ one takes $G_n=\Lambda_n$, the sequences supported on the first $n$ coordinates, and $m=L^d$; the blocks are finite and the volumes are cardinalities $m^n$. For the p-adic integers one takes $G=\mathbb Z_p$ and $G_n=p^n\mathbb Z_p$; then $[G_n:G_{n+1}]=p$, the blocks are the p-adic balls of radius $p^{-n}$, and the normalised Haar measure gives them volume $p^{-n}$. In both cases $G_n/G_{n+1}$ has $m$ elements and a block of level $n$ has $m^n$ children of level $0$.

    A model on such a system is a spin system with coupling $J(x-y)=J_k$ for $\operatorname{lev}(x-y)=k$, exactly as in the decimation article; the prior is a fixed probability measure on the single-spin space, and the Gibbs measure on a block is normalised with counting measure or Haar measure according to which case one is in. When $G$ is uncountable, $\mathbb P_\beta$ is a measure on $S^{G}$ for a countable set $S$ of blocks; the statements below are about a block, which is a finite-index subgroup, and are unaffected.
