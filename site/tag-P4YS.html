    Dilation covariance of the decimation operator

    proposition · P4YS · in The renormalisation group map

    For $\lambda>0$, with $\lambda_*\mu$ the law of $\lambda s$ under $\mu$,

    $$\mathcal T_b(\lambda_*\mu)=\lambda_*\bigl(\mathcal T_{b\lambda^2}\mu\bigr),\qquad \mathcal R_{\theta,b}(\lambda_*\mu)=\lambda_*\bigl(\mathcal R_{\theta,b\lambda^2}\mu\bigr).$$
    Proof of dilation covariance

    Sampling $s_a$ from $\lambda_*\mu$ is the same as writing $s_a=\lambda t_a$ with $t_a$ sampled independently from $\mu$. The exponent becomes $b\lambda^2\sum_{a<a'}t_at_{a'}$, the normaliser becomes $Z_{b\lambda^2}(\mu)$, and the sum becomes $\lambda\sum_at_a$. Hence the law of $\sum_as_a$ is the image under multiplication by $\lambda$ of the law of $\sum_at_a$ computed with coupling $b\lambda^2$, which is the first identity. Multiplying by $L^{-\theta}$ afterwards gives the second, since dilations commute.

    The identity is the reason no separate analysis is needed at each scale: a factor $\lambda$ in the spin is a factor $\lambda^2$ in the coupling, so the pair $(\text{scale},\text{coupling})$ flows on a cone.
