    Figures and simulations

    8 generated figures, 7 interactive ones

    Every figure and every interactive panel in the wiki, with what it computes, the articles that show it, and what checks that the computation is right. The distinction in the last line is the one this wiki cares about: a figure computed from the model can be checked by a script, and a schematic cannot. The schematics are labelled as such rather than dressed up, and the rest say which test would catch them being wrong.
