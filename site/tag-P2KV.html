    Effective potential of a decimation step

    definition · P2KV · in The phi-fourth hierarchical model and its truncations

    For $b\geq0$ the effective potential $\mathcal V_bV$ is defined by

    $$e^{-(\mathcal V_bV)(s)}=\frac{1}{Z_b(V)}\int_{\mathbb R^{m-1}}\exp\Bigl(b\sum_{a<a'}s_as_{a'}\Bigr)\prod_{a=1}^{m-1}e^{-V(s_a)}\,ds_1\cdots ds_{m-1},$$

    with $s_m=s-\sum_{a<m}s_a$, and the scaled potential at exponent $\theta$ is $(\mathcal R_\theta V)(s)=(\mathcal V_bV)(L^{\theta}s)$ up to an additive constant. The potential formulation and the law formulation of the map article carry the same information: $e^{-(\mathcal V_bV)}$ is the density of $\mathcal T_b(e^{-V}ds)$.

    The constant in front is fixed by normalisation and plays no role in the flow of the shape of the potential; additive constants are the free energy increments of the free energy recursion and are dropped from the potential from here on.

    ## The Gaussian truncation {#section-gaussian}

    The one family the recursion preserves is the quadratic one, and on it the recursion is explicit.
