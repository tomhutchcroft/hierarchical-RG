    Self-similar form of the block-spin recursion

    theorem · T3GD · in The renormalisation group map

    Let $\beta\geq0$, $\theta_0=\tfrac12(d+\alpha)$ and $\nu_n$ be the law of $L^{-\theta_0n}S_{\Lambda_n}$ under $\mathbb P_n$ of the hierarchical model. Then, with $b=\beta J_1=\beta JL^{-(d+\alpha)}$,

    $$\nu_n=\mathcal R_{\theta_0,b}^{\,n}\nu_0\qquad(n\geq0).$$
    Proof of the autonomous form

    The decimation identity gives $\mu_n=\mathcal T_{\beta J_n}\mu_{n-1}$ with $J_n=J_1L^{-(d+\alpha)(n-1)}$. Let $s_a$ have law $\mu_{n-1}$ and write $s_a=L^{\theta_0(n-1)}t_a$, so that $t_a$ has law $\nu_{n-1}$. Then $\beta J_n\sum_{a<a'}s_as_{a'}=\beta J_nL^{2\theta_0(n-1)}\sum_{a<a'}t_at_{a'}$, and $2\theta_0=d+\alpha$ gives $\beta J_nL^{2\theta_0(n-1)}=\beta J_1$. Hence $\mathcal T_{\beta J_n}\mu_{n-1}$ is the image under multiplication by $L^{\theta_0(n-1)}$ of $\mathcal T_{\beta J_1}\nu_{n-1}$, and $S_{\Lambda_n}=\sum_as_a=L^{\theta_0(n-1)}\sum_at_a$. Multiplying by $L^{-\theta_0n}$ gives $\nu_n=\mathcal R_{\theta_0,\beta J_1}\nu_{n-1}$. Induction from $\nu_0$ gives the statement.

    The exponent $\theta_0$ is not a physical prediction but the unique choice for which the coupling does not depend on $n$: with any other $\theta$ the argument of $\mathcal T$ would be $\beta J_1L^{2(\theta-\theta_0)(n-1)}$, growing or decaying geometrically, and the composition would not be a power of one map. Two consequences follow, and both are used later. The trajectory is the orbit of the initial law $\nu_0$ under a single map, so its asymptotics are those of a dynamical system; and if the orbit converges to a limit $\nu^*$ with $0<\int s^2d\nu^*<\infty$, then $\mathbb E[S_{\Lambda_n}^2]=L^{2\theta_0n}\int s^2d\nu^*\,(1+o(1))$, which fixes the growth of the block spin and hence one exponent, as the exponent article shows.
