    Uniform direction and independence for rotation invariant laws

    proposition · N4TZ · in Hierarchical models with continuous symmetry

    Let $\nu$ be a rotation invariant probability measure on $\mathbb R^r$ with $\nu(\{0\})=0$, $r\geq2$. Then under $\nu$ the random variable $S/\lvert S\rvert$ is uniform on $\mathbb S^{r-1}$ and independent of $\lvert S\rvert$. In particular the conclusion holds for every law of the Gaussian orbit and for every rotation invariant fixed point of $\mathcal R_{\theta,b}$.

    Proof

    Let $f$ be bounded measurable on $\mathbb S^{r-1}$ and $g$ bounded measurable on $[0,\infty)$. Since $\lvert QS\rvert=\lvert S\rvert$ for orthogonal $Q$ and $\nu$ is invariant,

    $$\int f(S/\lvert S\rvert)g(\lvert S\rvert)\,d\nu(S)=\int f(QS/\lvert QS\rvert)g(\lvert S\rvert)\,d\nu(S)$$

    for every $Q$. Averaging over $Q$ against the Haar measure of $O(r)$ and using Fubini, the right-hand side is $\int \bar f\,g(\lvert S\rvert)d\nu(S)$ with $\bar f$ the average of $f$ over the sphere, which is $\int f\,d\sigma$ for the uniform $\sigma$. Taking $g=\mathbf 1_{B}$ for a Borel $B\subseteq[0,\infty)$ gives $\int f(S/\lvert S\rvert)\mathbf 1_B(\lvert S\rvert)d\nu=\bigl(\int f\,d\sigma\bigr)\nu(\lvert S\rvert\in B)$, which is the product statement; the case $f\equiv1$ gives uniformity.

    The proposition explains why "the direction is uniform" is not by itself a critical phenomenon: it holds at every temperature in the Gaussian model. What Bleher and Major prove is that it holds at the critical temperature of the interacting model as well, where the direction could have concentrated, and that this coexists with a vanishing spontaneous magnetisation.

    ## Dyson's conjecture and the Thouless-like effect {#section-thouless}

    At the marginal exponent the interaction is summable exactly when the level couplings are, and the question is whether summability is the same as the absence of a transition. Dyson conjectured that it is, and Bleher and Major proved the conjecture for the vector model.
