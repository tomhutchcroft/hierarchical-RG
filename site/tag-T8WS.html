    Even diagonal moments of a child increase under the decimation tilt

    proposition · T8WS · in Critical exponents from the renormalisation group

    Let $\nu$ be a symmetric law on $\mathbb R$, let $S_1,\dots,S_m$ be independent with law $\nu$, and let $\mathbb E_{\mathrm{tilt}}$ be the expectation under the normalised measure $\propto e^{b\sum_{a<a'}S_aS_{a'}}\prod_a\nu(dS_a)$, $b\geq0$. Then $\mathbb E_{\mathrm{tilt}}[S_1^{2r}]\geq\int s^{2r}\nu(ds)$ for every $r\geq1$.

    Proof

    With $T=\sum_{a\geq2}S_a$ and $W_0=\exp(b\sum_{2\leq a<a'}S_aS_{a'})$, the conditional weight of $S_1=x$ is $f(x)=\mathbb E_0[e^{bxT}W_0]=\sum_k\frac{b^{2k}}{(2k)!}\mathbb E_0[T^{2k}W_0]x^{2k}$, with no odd terms since flipping blocks $2,\dots,m$ together leaves $W_0$ fixed and sends $T$ to $-T$. All coefficients are nonnegative, so $f$ and $x^{2r}$ are nondecreasing in $x^2$, and Chebyshev's association inequality for the single variable $S_1^2$ gives the claim after dividing by $\mathbb E_0[f(S_1)]$. FKG cannot be used directly, since $S_1^2$ is not an increasing function of the spins.
