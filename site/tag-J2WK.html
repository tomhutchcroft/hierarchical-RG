    Children of a block and the top level between them

    theorem · J2WK · in The hierarchical lattice

    For $n\geq1$,

    $$\Lambda_n=\bigsqcup_{a\in A}\bigl(e_n(a)+\Lambda_{n-1}\bigr),$$

    the pieces are the blocks of radius $L^{n-1}$ contained in $\Lambda_n$, each is a translate of $\Lambda_{n-1}$, and $e_n(a)$ has level $n$. If $x$ and $y$ lie in different pieces then $\lVert x-y\rVert=L^n$, whatever the distance between the two pieces as sets.

    Proof of the child decomposition

    Let $z\in\Lambda_n$ and $a=z_n$. Then $z-e_n(a)$ has vanishing $n$-th coordinate and vanishes above $n$ because $z$ does, so $z-e_n(a)\in\Lambda_{n-1}$ and $z\in e_n(a)+\Lambda_{n-1}$. If $z\in e_n(a)+\Lambda_{n-1}$ then $z_n=a$, so the pieces for distinct $a$ are disjoint, which gives the union. Since $e_n(a)\in\Lambda_n$ and $\Lambda_n$ is a subgroup, $e_n(a)+\Lambda_{n-1}$ is a coset of $\Lambda_{n-1}$ inside $\Lambda_n$; the blocks of radius $L^{n-1}$ are exactly the cosets of $\Lambda_{n-1}$, and by the previous section each is contained in or disjoint from $\Lambda_n$. Finally $\operatorname{lev}(e_n(a))=n$ because the $n$-th coordinate is the last nonzero one.

    For the last claim let $x=e_n(a)+u$ and $y=e_n(b)+v$ with $u,v\in\Lambda_{n-1}$ and $a\neq b$. Then $x-y=e_n(a-b)+(u-v)$, and $u-v\in\Lambda_{n-1}$ vanishes at coordinate $n$, so the $n$-th coordinate of $x-y$ is $a-b\neq0$ and no coordinate above $n$ is nonzero. Hence $\operatorname{lev}(x-y)=n$.

    The count is consistent: $\lvert A\rvert\cdot\lvert\Lambda_{n-1}\rvert=m\cdot m^{n-1}=m^n=\lvert\Lambda_n\rvert$.

    The theorem is what a decimation step needs, since a pair chosen from two different children of $\Lambda_n$ has the same level $n$ whatever the vertices, so a kernel that is constant on levels assigns the same intensity to all $m(m-1)/2$ pairs of distinct children, and the energy of the block depends on the children only through their sums.

    ## Power kernels {#section-kernels}

    A kernel is a nonnegative symmetric function $J:\mathbb H^d_L\to[0,\infty)$ with $J(0)$ irrelevant for the graph. The power kernel at exponent $\alpha$ is $J(x)=\lVert x\rVert^{-d-\alpha}$ off the origin, and its level-$k$ value is $J_k=L^{-(d+\alpha)k}$, so it charges each pair of the level-$k$ shell by one constant.
