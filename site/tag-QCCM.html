    The plus block spin is pinned at least at the mean-field rate, the free one fluctuates at least that much

    theorem · QCCM · in Critical exponents from the renormalisation group

    (i) The block average of the plus state concentrates: with $Y_n=S_{\Lambda_n}/m^n$,

    $$\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{+}}(Y_n)\leq\frac{L^{-(d-\alpha)n}}{\beta C},$$

    so its fluctuations vanish at least like $L^{-(d-\alpha)n/2}$, and the plus block spin never grows faster than the mean-field rate, $2\theta_+\leq d+\alpha$.

    (ii) The free block average does not concentrate faster than the mean-field rate: $\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{\mathrm{free}}}(Y_n)\geq M_n(\beta)L^{-(d-\alpha)n}/(\beta C)$.

    (iii) Suppose that at the critical intensity the rescaled orbit converges with a second moment $v^*=\int s^2d\nu^*\in(0,\infty)$, as in the growth theorem, and that the transition is continuous, so that $M_n(\beta_c)\to0$. Then the free variance is $L^{2\theta_0n}v^*(1+o(1))$ and the sandwich gives

    $$M_n\leq\beta Cv^*(1+o(1)),\qquad\frac{\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{+}}(Y_n)}{\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{\mathrm{free}}}(Y_n)}\leq\frac{M_n}{v^*}\longrightarrow0:$$

    at criticality a boundary field of size $B_n\to0$ still pins the block spin, and the ratio of the two fluctuations measures the finite-size magnetisation.

    Proof

    (i) Divide the lower half of the sandwich by $m^{2n}$ and use $M_n\leq1$, which holds because $\lvert\sigma_x\rvert\leq1$.

    (ii) Divide the upper half by $m^{2n}$.

    (iii) Under the hypothesis of the growth theorem the free variance is $L^{2\theta_0n}v^*(1+o(1))$, and the upper half of the sandwich is $M_n\leq\beta C\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{\mathrm{free}}}(S_{\Lambda_n})/L^{2\theta_0n}$. The ratio bound divides the two halves of the sandwich by each other, and $M_n\to0$ is the continuity hypothesis, which is where the vanishing of the ratio comes from.

    The third consequence is the one worth reading twice. The free block spin at criticality has a genuine scaling limit, so it fluctuates over a range of order $L^{\theta_0n}$; the plus block spin, whose boundary field is only $B_n=CL^{-\alpha n}$ — vanishing, and at a rate that makes the boundary energy of a block grow — fluctuates over a range that is a vanishing fraction of that. The mechanism is the one the identity of the block-average section describes: the boundary injects magnetisation into the block, and the injected magnetisation is what the finite-size $M_n$ measures.

    What this route does not give is the value of $\kappa$. The lower half of the sandwich bounds the free variance below, in terms of $M_n$, and $M_n\to0$ at criticality, so it cannot certify a growth of order $L^{2\theta_0n}$; the upper half bounds the plus variance, not the free one. That is not an accident of the estimates: the two variances differ by the factor between them, which is exactly what distinguishes a pinned block spin from a fluctuating one. Certifying $\kappa=\alpha$ therefore still needs either the convergence of the rescaled orbit, which is the growth theorem, or an upper bound on the free block-spin variance which this argument does not supply.
