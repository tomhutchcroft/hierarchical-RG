    Hierarchical RG

    A wiki on the renormalisation group on hierarchical lattices: 17 articles, 6 figures, 62 tagged assertions.

    The renormalisation group is exact on a hierarchical lattice. A block has no boundary, every pair between two children carries one coupling, and a decimation step is an operation on the law of a single number. This wiki develops that structure — the lattice, the recursion, the fixed points, the exponents, the literature — with complete proofs where they are short and precise attribution where they are not.

    Start here for the argument and four reading paths, or go straight to part one.
