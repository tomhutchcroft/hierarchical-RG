    Continuity of decimation on compactly supported laws

    proposition · E7NM · in The renormalisation group map

    Fix $K<\infty$ and $B<\infty$. The map $(\mu,b)\mapsto\mathcal T_b\mu$ is continuous on the set of laws supported in $[-K,K]$ and couplings $b\in[0,B]$, for the topology of weak convergence, and $\mathcal T_b$ maps that set into the laws supported in $[-mK,mK]$.

    Proof of continuity

    On $[-K,K]^m$ the tilt $\exp(b\sum_{a<a'}s_as_{a'})$ is continuous in $(s,b)$ and bounded above by $e^{Bm(m-1)K^2/2}$ and below by $e^{-Bm(m-1)K^2/2}$, and the sum $\sum_as_a$ is continuous and bounded by $mK$. If $\mu_j\Rightarrow\mu$ weakly with all $\mu_j$ supported in $[-K,K]$ and $b_j\to b$, then $\mu_j^{\otimes m}\Rightarrow\mu^{\otimes m}$ because the product of a weakly convergent sequence of laws on a compact set converges weakly to the product, and the integrands above are continuous and bounded, so $Z_{b_j}(\mu_j)\to Z_b(\mu)$. The normalisers are bounded below by $e^{-Bm(m-1)K^2/2}$, which is positive, so the normalised measures converge weakly by the same bounded convergence applied to $\mathbf 1_{A'}(\sum_as_a)$ tested against bounded continuous functions of $\sum_as_a$. The support statement is immediate from $\lvert s_a\rvert\leq K$.

    The hypothesis of compact support is met by the Ising prior at finite volume, where the block spin of $\Lambda_n$ lies in $[-m^n,m^n]$, and it is met after rescaling only in the sense that the support bound $L^{(d-\theta)n}$ grows with $n$ when $\theta<d$. The rescaled orbit is therefore not confined to a fixed compact set, and the continuity above does not by itself give compactness: what compactness requires is a uniform bound on the second moment of the rescaled laws, and supplying that bound is where the analysis of a particular model enters. Below the critical intensity the block spin has typical size $L^{dn/2}$, above it $L^{dn}$, and at the critical intensity $L^{\theta_0n}$ with $\theta_0=\tfrac12(d+\alpha)$; the rescaled orbit therefore shrinks, grows, or stays of order one in the three cases.

    ## The autonomous form {#section-autonomous}

    The model applies a different operator at each scale. Rescaling the spin makes the sequence a power of one map, and this is the precise sense in which the renormalisation group is a dynamical system here.
