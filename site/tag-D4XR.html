    Existence of a complete trajectory in three dimensions

    theorem · D4XR · in Abdesselam's trajectory between two fixed points

    Let the field be three-dimensional with a modified free propagator, and let the interaction be a $\varphi^4$ perturbation with a small coupling. Then there is a sequence of effective potentials $(V_j)_{j\in\mathbb Z}$ satisfying the renormalisation recursion exactly, asymptotic to the ultraviolet Gaussian fixed point as $j\to-\infty$ and to the nontrivial infrared fixed point as $j\to+\infty$. The construction is nonperturbative in the sense that no order-by-order expansion is resummed: it produces the trajectory as the solution of a fixed-point equation in a Banach space of sequences.

    The statement is Theorem 1 of Abdesselam [@abdesselam-2007], with the summary of the method and of the role of the modified propagator in the six-page account of the same construction [@abdesselam-2009]. The infrared endpoint is an analogue of the Wilson-Fisher fixed point: it is the nontrivial fixed point of the model with the modified propagator rather than a statement about the unmodified three-dimensional $\varphi^4$ theory, whose renormalisation-group trajectory is constructed by different means in the work of Brydges, Mitter and Scoppola [@brydges-mitter-scoppola-2003].
