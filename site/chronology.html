    42 works, 1967–2025

    The works of the catalogue in the order they appeared, with the articles that cite each. The bibliography answers the question "what is this work and where is it used"; this page answers the other one, which is what was available at each stage of the subject. The layers are visible: the model and its first analysis in the 1960s and 1970s, the correlation inequalities alongside them, the renormalisation-group analysis of the hierarchical model in the 1980s, and the percolation results of the last decade.
