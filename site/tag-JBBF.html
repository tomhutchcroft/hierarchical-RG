    At alpha = d summability is decided by the level sequence alone

    proposition · JBBF · in The two marginal exponents

    Let $\alpha=d$. Then

    $$\sum_{x\neq0}J(\lVert x\rVert)=(1-L^{-d})\sum_{k\geq1}l_k,$$

    so the interaction is summable if and only if $\sum_kl_k<\infty$, and it is summable for every $\alpha>d$ and for no $\alpha

    Proof

    The shell at level $k$ has $(1-L^{-d})L^{dk}$ vertices by the shell count, and each contributes $J_k=l_kL^{-(d+\alpha)k}$. At $\alpha=d$ the two powers of $L$ cancel and the shell contributes $(1-L^{-d})l_k$, so the sum is the stated multiple of $\sum_kl_k$.
