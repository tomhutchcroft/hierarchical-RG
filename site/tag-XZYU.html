    The block-spin variance is maximal at zero boundary field, and the sandwich it gives

    proposition · XZYU · in Critical exponents from the renormalisation group

    For every $\beta$ and $n$, the map $t\mapsto\mathbb E_{\mathbb P^{(t)}_{\Lambda_n}}[S_{\Lambda_n}]$ is concave and increasing on $[0,1]$, so $t\mapsto\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{(t)}}(S_{\Lambda_n})$ is nonincreasing there; hence

    $$\frac{\beta C\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{+}}(S_{\Lambda_n})}{L^{2\theta_0n}}\ \leq\ M_n(\beta)\ \leq\ \frac{\beta C\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{\mathrm{free}}}(S_{\Lambda_n})}{L^{2\theta_0n}},$$

    where $M_n(\beta)=\mathbb E_{\mathbb P_{\Lambda_n}^{+}}[\sigma_x]$ is the finite-size magnetisation and $2\theta_0=d+\alpha$.

    Proof

    The two derivatives of the identity are $\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb E_{\mathbb P^{(t)}}[S_{\Lambda_n}]=\beta B_n\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{(t)}}(S_{\Lambda_n})$ and

    $$\frac{d^2}{dt^2}\mathbb E_{\mathbb P^{(t)}}[S_{\Lambda_n}]=(\beta B_n)^2\sum_{x,y,z\in\Lambda_n}\mathbb E_{\mathbb P^{(t)}}\bigl[\sigma_x;\sigma_y;\sigma_z\bigr],$$

    the sum of third cumulants, which is nonpositive by the GHS inequality for ferromagnetic Ising spins in a nonnegative field [@ghs-1970]. So the mean is concave, its derivative is nonincreasing, and by the previous display so is the variance. The variance is even in $t$, because $\mathbb P^{(-t)}$ is the image of $\mathbb P^{(t)}$ under the global flip, so the integral of the variance over $[-1,1]$ lies between twice its value at $t=1$ and twice its value at $t=0$. Multiplying the identity $m^nM_n(\beta)=\tfrac12\beta B_n\int_{-1}^{1}\operatorname{Var}_{\mathbb P^{(t)}}(S_{\Lambda_n})\,dt$ by these bounds and using $B_n=CL^{-\alpha n}$ together with $L^{2\theta_0n}=m^nL^{\alpha n}$ gives the sandwich.

    Three consequences follow, and none of them needs the orbit to converge.
