    Exactness of decimation is a property of the block geometry

    proposition · X5QM · in Hierarchical and Euclidean renormalisation

    Suppose that a block $B$ is the disjoint union of $m$ children $B_1,\ldots,B_m$ and that the interaction is a sum over unordered pairs of a coupling that is constant on the set of pairs joining two given children. Then the energy of a configuration on $B$ is a function of the child configurations through the $m$ block sums alone, and the law of the $m$-tuple of block sums under the Gibbs measure of $B$ is determined by the interaction inside a child and by the $m(m-1)/2$ couplings between children. If in addition every child is isomorphic to $B$ with the same internal interaction, one decimation step is the map $\mathcal T_b$ of the map article, and the family of interactions on the scale of a block is closed.

    Proof of the exactness criterion

    By hypothesis every pair $\{x,y\}$ with $x\in B_i$, $y\in B_j$, $i\neq j$, carries the same coupling $b_{ij}$, so its contribution to the energy is $-b_{ij}\sigma_x\sigma_y$ and the sum over such pairs is $-b_{ij}S_iS_j$ with $S_i=\sum_{x\in B_i}\sigma_x$. The pairs inside a child contribute the internal energy of that child, and no other pairs exist. Hence the total energy is $\sum_iH_{B_i}(\sigma^{(i)})-\sum_{i<j}b_{ij}S_iS_j$, a function of the child configurations through the sums and of the internal energies. Dividing by the partition function and integrating over the internal configurations at fixed sums gives the stated dependence; if all children are isomorphic and $b_{ij}=b$ for all $i\neq j$, this is the tilt-plus-summation of $\mathcal T_b$.

    The hypothesis of the proposition is what the child decomposition provides. Every pair of distinct children of $\Lambda_n$ is joined by pairs of the same level $n$, so $b_{ij}=J_n$ for all $i\neq j$; every child is a translate of $\Lambda_{n-1}$, so the internal interaction is the same function; and the block at level $n$ is the block at level $1$ with the scale changed, which is the self-similarity that lets one map serve at every scale.
